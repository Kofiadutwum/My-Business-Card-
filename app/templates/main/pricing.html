What happens when a plan expires

The card stops resolving publicly and anyone opening the link sees a short notice instead. Your details are not deleted. Signing in and renewing puts the same card back at the same link, and contacts already saved on other people's phones are unaffected — that file lives on their device now.

There is a {{ config.GRACE_PERIOD_DAYS }}-day grace period after the expiry date before the card goes dark.
